# Custom Executive Slot

Status: Product decision recorded for later implementation.

## Decision
MAYA will reserve one blank, user-buildable executive/agent slot in addition to the standard executive team.

The slot should let a user define a role that MAYA does not ship with by default. The user may shape its name, title, hard goal, domain, working style, capabilities, preferred outputs, and approval boundaries while MAYA preserves platform-level governance and safety rules.

## Design intent
- One intentionally blank executive slot is always available to the user.
- The user creates the role from concept and purpose first; MAYA helps translate that into a durable executive chassis.
- User configuration may specialize the role but must not weaken system-level security, privacy, authorization, evidence, or action boundaries.
- The custom role should participate in multi-executive conversations and MAYA adjudication like other executives while preserving its own role fidelity and material dissent.
- The role remains editable and upgradeable over time without modifying the core MAYA executive set.
- The slot is not a generic prompt box. It should become a persistent, named role with memory/context boundaries, explicit capabilities, and approval gates.

## MIMI — deferred
Gallery Director concept is intentionally deferred until after the core executive foundation is stable. Name reserved: MIMI.

MIMI's future north star: identify where the artist's authentic work genuinely matters, strengthen and document the body of work without imitation or flattery, preserve the artist's freedom to evolve, and support serious curatorial presentation and career positioning.
